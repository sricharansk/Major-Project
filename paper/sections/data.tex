\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}
All sources are public (Table~\ref{tab:sources}). Speed targets come from the NYC DOT Real-Time Traffic Speed Data
feed (Socrata \texttt{i4gi-tjb9}), restricted to Manhattan links with geometry, for 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-20. Each
record carries a floating local timestamp that is localised to \texttt{America/New\_York} and converted to UTC; the
ambiguous daylight-saving hour is nulled rather than guessed. Records are snapped to a right-labelled 5-minute grid
per link, averaged within a bin, bounded to physically plausible speeds, and forward-filled causally for at most
three steps. Context comes from Open-Meteo (historical forecasts for the operational track, the finalised archive
for the retrospective track), NYC 311 service requests with a declared 24-hour lag, Motor Vehicle Collisions
(retrospective only; the feed lagged publication by about four months at download time) and NYC permitted events.
Sampled automated traffic volume counts parameterise simulation demand only. OpenStreetMap supplies geometry for
the simulated corridor. Every download is logged with URL, time, HTTP status and SHA-256, and a server-side audit of
dataset identity and observed date ranges precedes every download; the full-year link enumeration timed out
server-side, so the link set is the 125 links citywide active since 2026-09-20, of which 26 are in Manhattan. Of
1{,}968{,}303 raw Manhattan speed records, 978{,}018 carry a zero speed (the feed's no-measurement encoding) and are
treated as missing, never imputed; 990{,}226 valid records fill a 84{,}084-step grid over 19 links (observed fraction
0.611), seven links being dead feeds with no valid 2026 record. Links with at least 50\,\% training-period coverage
enter the models: 16 links, three of which have no valid test-period observation and are reported as
NOT\_EVALUATED (\texttt{reports/data\_audit/link\_coverage\_operational.csv},
\texttt{reports/experiments/link\_test\_support\_operational.csv}).
