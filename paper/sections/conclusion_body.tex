We presented a complete, reproducible prototype that joins multi-horizon graph-transformer forecasting, conformal
calibration, model-faithful explanations, allowlisted retrieval, a locally hosted language model constrained by
deterministic checks, and matched-seed simulation into one auditable advisory workflow on public New York City data.
Conclusions about accuracy, calibration and advisory quality are drawn only from the generated tables and are added
when the real-data run completes. Future work includes multi-seed evaluation, a denser sensor network, calibrated
simulation demand from continuous counts, pedestrian modelling, and an externally reviewed retrieval benchmark.
